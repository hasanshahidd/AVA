    Welcome to CAIVA setup

    The installer has prepared this server. Review the system check below, then configure your organization. Setup takes a few minutes and runs entirely on this server.

    Organization

    Identifies your organization inside the platform and creates the first administrator.

    Derived from the organization name. Lowercase letters and digits, 2–31 characters, starting with a letter. Cannot be changed after installation.

    Sign-in name of the first administrator. A one-time password is generated at the end of setup.
