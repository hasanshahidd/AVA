# Human Resources Security Policy

## Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to establish a framework for managing human resources security within the healthcare entity. This policy aims to protect sensitive information and ensure that security measures are integrated into the employment lifecycle, from recruitment through termination.

## Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party personnel who have access to the healthcare entity’s information systems and sensitive data. It encompasses all aspects of employment, including recruitment, onboarding, ongoing employment, and termination processes.

## Policy Statements
1. **Recruitment and Selection**  
   All candidates for employment shall undergo background checks and reference verification as appropriate, based on their role and access to sensitive information.

2. **Employee Onboarding**  
   New employees shall receive security awareness training as part of their onboarding process. This training will cover the entity’s security policies, procedures, and their responsibilities regarding information security.

3. **Ongoing Employment**  
   Employees shall be regularly trained on security policies and procedures. Performance evaluations will include assessments of adherence to security practices.

4. **Termination Procedures**  
   Upon termination of employment, whether voluntary or involuntary, immediate measures will be taken to revoke access to all information systems and retrieve any entity-owned assets. An exit interview will be conducted to remind the employee of their ongoing confidentiality obligations.

5. **Documentation and Record Keeping**  
   All security-related activities, including background checks, training completion, and termination processes, shall be documented and securely maintained for audit purposes.

## Responsibilities
- **Human Resources Department**  
  Responsible for implementing and enforcing this policy, conducting background checks, and ensuring that all employees complete required training.

- **IT Department**  
  Responsible for managing access controls and ensuring that access to information systems is promptly revoked upon termination of employment.

- **All Employees**  
  Responsible for adhering to the policies and procedures outlined in this document and reporting any security concerns to their supervisor or the designated security officer.

## Review & Monitoring
This policy shall be reviewed annually or as needed in response to changes in regulatory requirements or organizational structure. Compliance with this policy will be monitored through regular audits and assessments of human resources processes. Non-compliance will be addressed according to the entity’s disciplinary procedures.
