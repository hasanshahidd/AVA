# Background Verification and Asset Management Policy

## Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to establish a framework for conducting background verification checks on all candidates for employment, contractors, and third-party users, as well as to ensure the recovery of all organizational assets upon termination of employment, contract, or agreement. This policy aims to protect the integrity, confidentiality, and availability of the healthcare entity's resources and information.

## Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party users who have access to the healthcare entity's facilities, systems, and sensitive information. It encompasses all stages of the employment lifecycle, including recruitment, onboarding, and offboarding processes.

## Policy Statements
1. **Background Verification Checks**  
   a. The healthcare entity shall conduct comprehensive background verification checks on all candidates for employment, contractors, and third-party users prior to granting access to organizational resources.  
   b. Background checks shall include, but are not limited to, criminal history, employment history, education verification, and professional licenses as applicable.  
   c. All background checks shall comply with relevant federal, state, and local laws and regulations.

2. **Asset Recovery**  
   a. All organizational assets, including but not limited to, physical equipment, access cards, and electronic devices, must be returned upon termination of employment, contract, or agreement.  
   b. The responsibility for asset recovery lies with the immediate supervisor or designated personnel, who must ensure that all assets are accounted for before the final separation of the individual from the organization.  
   c. Failure to return organizational assets may result in legal action and/or financial liability.

## Responsibilities
- **Human Resources (HR)**: Responsible for implementing and overseeing the background verification process, ensuring compliance with applicable laws, and maintaining records of background checks.
- **Supervisors/Managers**: Responsible for coordinating asset recovery during the offboarding process and ensuring that all organizational assets are returned.
- **IT Department**: Responsible for disabling access to electronic systems and retrieving electronic devices upon termination.
- **Compliance Officer**: Responsible for monitoring adherence to this policy and conducting periodic audits to assess effectiveness.

## Review & Monitoring
This policy shall be reviewed annually or as necessary to ensure its effectiveness and compliance with applicable laws and regulations. The Compliance Officer will monitor adherence to this policy and report any violations or areas for improvement to senior management. Audit logs related to background checks and asset recovery shall be maintained for a minimum of three years.
