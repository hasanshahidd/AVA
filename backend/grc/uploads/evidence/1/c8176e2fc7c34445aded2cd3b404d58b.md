# Human Resources Security Policy

## Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to establish a framework for managing security aspects related to employment and termination within the healthcare entity. This policy aims to protect sensitive information, ensure compliance with applicable regulations, and maintain a secure working environment for all personnel.

## Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and temporary staff of the healthcare entity, including those who have access to sensitive information and systems. It encompasses all stages of the employment lifecycle, from recruitment to termination.

## Policy Statements
1. **Pre-Employment Screening**  
   All candidates for employment shall undergo background checks, reference checks, and verification of qualifications to ensure they meet the security requirements of their roles.

2. **Security Awareness Training**  
   All employees must complete mandatory security awareness training during onboarding and annually thereafter. This training will cover data protection, privacy policies, and incident reporting procedures.

3. **Access Control**  
   Access to sensitive information and systems will be granted based on the principle of least privilege. Role-based access controls will be implemented to ensure employees have access only to the information necessary for their job functions.

4. **Termination Procedures**  
   Upon termination of employment, whether voluntary or involuntary, immediate steps will be taken to revoke access to all systems and retrieve any company property. An exit interview will be conducted to remind the departing employee of their ongoing confidentiality obligations.

5. **Incident Reporting**  
   Employees are required to report any security incidents or breaches immediately to their supervisor or the designated security officer. Failure to report incidents may result in disciplinary action.

## Responsibilities
- **Human Resources Department**: Responsible for implementing pre-employment screening, conducting exit interviews, and maintaining employee records related to security training and access controls.
- **IT Department**: Responsible for managing access controls and ensuring timely revocation of access upon termination.
- **All Employees**: Responsible for adhering to this policy, completing required training, and reporting any security incidents.

## Review & Monitoring
This policy will be reviewed annually by the Human Resources and IT departments to ensure its effectiveness and compliance with applicable laws and regulations. Monitoring will include audits of employee access rights, training completion rates, and incident reports to identify areas for improvement.
