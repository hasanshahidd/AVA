# Background Verification and Asset Management Policy

## Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to ensure that the healthcare entity maintains a secure and trustworthy workforce by conducting thorough background verification checks on all candidates for employment, contractors, and third-party users. Additionally, this policy outlines the procedures for the recovery of organizational assets upon termination of employment, contracts, or agreements.

## Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party users engaged with the healthcare entity. It encompasses all stages of the employment lifecycle, from recruitment to termination, and includes all organizational assets, both physical and digital.

## Policy Statements
1. **Background Verification Checks**  
   a. The healthcare entity shall conduct comprehensive background verification checks on all candidates for employment, contractors, and third-party users prior to the commencement of their roles.  
   b. Background checks shall include, but are not limited to, verification of identity, employment history, criminal record checks, and education verification, in compliance with applicable laws and regulations.  
   c. The results of background checks will be evaluated in accordance with the entity's hiring criteria and relevant legal standards.

2. **Asset Recovery Procedures**  
   a. Upon termination of employment, contract, or agreement, the healthcare entity shall ensure the recovery of all organizational assets, including but not limited to keys, identification badges, electronic devices, and proprietary information.  
   b. A formal exit process shall be established to facilitate the return of assets, which includes a checklist to be completed by the departing individual and their supervisor.  
   c. Failure to return organizational assets may result in legal action and/or financial liability.

## Responsibilities
- **Human Resources Department**: Responsible for conducting background verification checks and maintaining records of all checks performed. HR will also oversee the exit process and ensure asset recovery is completed.
- **Hiring Managers**: Responsible for ensuring that background checks are completed prior to hiring and for coordinating the asset recovery process with departing employees, contractors, or third-party users.
- **IT Department**: Responsible for managing the return of electronic assets and ensuring that access to digital systems is revoked upon termination.
- **Compliance Officer**: Responsible for ensuring that the policy is implemented in accordance with relevant laws and regulations and for conducting periodic audits of the process.

## Review & Monitoring
This policy shall be reviewed annually or as needed to ensure its effectiveness and compliance with applicable laws and regulations. The Compliance Officer will monitor adherence to this policy and report findings and recommendations to senior management. Any non-compliance will be addressed promptly to mitigate risks to the organization.
